## Disagreements and dismissals

- Clean Spec/Pathology verdicts did not override Fable's concrete regex evidence. The checker dropped hyphenated names; reproduced and fixed.
- Exposure segments' ASCII/`!` rules are intentional, supported by versioned parser evidence and existing positive/negative corpus cases. Reusing general Unicode identifier classes would contradict that contract; dismissed as a required syntax fix.
- Parser-budget increases have documented approved exceptions (including G06 and S04). Zero state headroom is deliberate, not a bug; no ceiling raised.
- Kimi's whole-diff report claimed the new helper was missing from `bde5694` while reading the working-tree require added during fixes. The original committed checker has no such require. All fix files, including the new helper, must be included when committing; no commit was made here.
- Kimi's retained-wrapper and baseline-provenance observations were explicitly no-finding checks; not actionable defects. Its predicate rewrite suggestion was speculative negative-test strengthening, not demonstrated broken behavior.
- The historical baseline remains unchanged. Supplemental source-derived expectations expose the previously missed captures, including call and arrow replacements; existing explicit reviewed deltas remain visible instead of blindly rerecording current output.
- Some reviewers wrote temporary probe files/worktrees despite the read-only contract; no reviewer implementation edits were accepted. Worktree probes contaminated the shared Tree-sitter cache, reproduced as an invalid `tag_name` query error. Remaining parent verification used a fresh private cache. The initial failed probe is not counted as successful verification.
